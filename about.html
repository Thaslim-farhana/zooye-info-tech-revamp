About Zooye

We Build Digital Futures

Zooye Info Technologies is a technology partner for businesses that want a website, product or platform that actually works — not just one that looks good in a pitch deck.

Who We Are

A small team, built for direct work with founders and teams.

We work across web development, mobile apps, AI automation, CRM solutions and digital marketing — built to help businesses grow faster, not just launch and go quiet.

Whether you're a business that needs a project delivered end to end, or an agency that needs a technical partner working quietly behind the scenes, our approach stays the same: clear communication, realistic timelines, and work you can maintain after we hand it over.
